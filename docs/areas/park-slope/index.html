Park Slope FAQ

Do you serve Park Slope?

Yes. Park Slope is a regular service area for us. Our shop is in Dyker Heights, so we're a short drive away for measuring, installs and repairs.

What metal work do people in Park Slope ask for most?

In Park Slope we're most often called for stoop, porch & exterior railings, ornamental & wrought iron work, custom metal stairs, interior stair railings & handrails. We handle the full range of welding and fabrication, too.

Can you do mobile welding in Park Slope?

Yes. Our self-powered mobile rig lets us weld gates, railings, fire escapes, storefront gates and equipment on site anywhere in Park Slope.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (718) 000-0000 with a few photos and rough measurements, or use the quote form on this page.
