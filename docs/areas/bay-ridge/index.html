Welding & metal fabrication in Bay Ridge, Brooklyn.

From the limestone row houses off Third Avenue to the larger homes on Colonial Road and Shore Road, Bay Ridge has some of the best old ironwork in Brooklyn. We're a few minutes away for railing restoration, gates, fire escapes and storefront gates on Third and Fifth Avenues.

Built for Bay Ridge buildings

What we see here: Limestone and brick row houses, Tudor and colonial-style detached homes, pre-war apartment buildings with fire escapes, and busy storefront corridors.

Every job starts with measuring on site, so ironwork fits Bay Ridge's real stoops, openings and slopes. We build in our Brooklyn shop and finish outdoor steel for NYC weather — with galvanizing or stainless recommended for salt-air exposure here.

Bay Ridge FAQ

Do you serve Bay Ridge?

Yes. Bay Ridge is a regular service area for us. Our shop is in Dyker Heights, so we're a short drive away for measuring, installs and repairs.

What metal work do people in Bay Ridge ask for most?

In Bay Ridge we're most often called for stoop, porch & exterior railings, ornamental & wrought iron work, fire escape repair & replacement, rolling gates & storefront security. We handle the full range of welding and fabrication, too.

Can you do mobile welding in Bay Ridge?

Yes. Our self-powered mobile rig lets us weld gates, railings, fire escapes, storefront gates and equipment on site anywhere in Bay Ridge.

How do I get a quote?
