    Built for Brooklyn

    Designed for old buildings and real weather.

    • Code-height railings and guards designed to NYC Building Code, with tight picket spacing.
    • Masonry anchoring done right — core-drilled or plated posts so stoop railings don't rot out at the base.
    • Outdoor finishes that last: hot-dip galvanizing, zinc-rich primer, industrial enamel or powder coat.
    • Fire-safety aware: fire-escape window gates, egress-friendly guards and FISP repair scopes.
    • Historic details matched — pickets, scrolls and castings copied so repairs disappear.

    The Angir method

    From photo to finished steel.

    Six steps, one shop, one crew. You always know what's happening and what it costs.

    1. 01

      Call or text photos

      Send a few photos and rough measurements. Most jobs get a price range the same day.

    2. 02

      We measure on site

      We laser-measure and template the real opening, stair or wall — Brooklyn buildings are never square.

    3. 03

      Drawing & fixed quote

      You approve a simple drawing, material and finish before any steel is cut. No surprises.

    4. 04

      Built in our shop

      Cut, fit and welded in our Dyker Heights shop by the same crew that installs it.

    5. 05

      Finished to last

      Primer and industrial paint, powder coat or hot-dip galvanizing — chosen for NYC weather.

    6. 06

      Install & walkthrough

      Clean, fast install. We check every weld, latch and anchor with you before we leave.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    All FAQs
    Where is Angir Welding & Fabrication located?

    Our shop is at 1363 81st St, Brooklyn, NY 11228, in Dyker Heights, close to Bay Ridge, Bensonhurst and Borough Park.

    What areas do you serve?

    All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.

    Do you do small welding jobs?

    Yes. A single broken hinge, a cracked bracket or a quick gate fix is welcome.

    Do you offer free estimates?

    Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

    Can you come to my location?

    Yes — our mobile welding rig is self-powered, so we can weld on site at your home, storefront, job site or yard.

    What metals do you work with?

    Carbon steel, stainless steel (304 and 316), aluminum and cast iron, using MIG, TIG and stick welding, plus torch and plasma cutting.

    How long does a custom railing or gate take?

    Most residential railings and gates are 1–3 weeks from approved design to install, depending on finish. Repairs are often same-week.
