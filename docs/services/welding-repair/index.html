Welding & Repair · Brooklyn & NYC

Welding repair, for anything that broke.

Cracked frames, snapped hinges, broken railings, split welds and worn parts — welded back stronger in our Brooklyn shop or on site with our mobile rig.

Call (718) 000-0000Free quote
  • Measured on site
  • Built in our Brooklyn shop
  • Written quote first

Most broken metal can be repaired for less than replacing it. We repair steel, stainless, aluminum and cast iron with MIG, TIG and stick processes — choosing the right one for the metal and the job.

Bring it to our Dyker Heights shop, or if it's attached to the building, we'll come to you.

What we do: welding repair

Common repairs

  • Broken gate hinges and latches
  • Cracked railings and loose posts
  • Furniture and frame repairs
  • Bike racks, carts and hand trucks
  • Machine guards, brackets and mounts
  • Equipment frames and stands

Processes

  • MIG (fast, strong on steel)
  • TIG (clean, precise, stainless & aluminum)
  • Stick (outdoors, rusty or thick steel)
  • Brazing and cast iron repair

How it works

  1. 1. Call or text photos

    Send a few photos and rough measurements. Most jobs get a price range the same day.

  2. 2. We measure on site

    We laser-measure and template the real opening, stair or wall — Brooklyn buildings are never square.

  3. 3. Drawing & fixed quote

    You approve a simple drawing, material and finish before any steel is cut. No surprises.

  4. 4. Built in our shop

    Cut, fit and welded in our Dyker Heights shop by the same crew that installs it.

  5. 5. Finished to last

    Primer and industrial paint, powder coat or hot-dip galvanizing — chosen for NYC weather.

  6. 6. Install & walkthrough

    Clean, fast install. We check every weld, latch and anchor with you before we leave.

Welding Repair FAQ

Is there a welding shop near me in Brooklyn?

We're at 1363 81st St in Dyker Heights, close to Bay Ridge, Bensonhurst, Borough Park and Sunset Park, and we travel throughout Brooklyn with mobile equipment.

How much does a small welding repair cost?

Small repairs are usually priced by the job. Send a photo and we can usually give you a price right away.

Can you weld aluminum and stainless?

Yes, with TIG welding.

Do you offer free estimates?

Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

What areas do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.
