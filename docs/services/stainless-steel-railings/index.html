Stairs & Railings · Brooklyn & NYC

Stainless, cable and glass railings.

Clean, modern railing systems in brushed stainless steel — with cable, rod or tempered glass infill — for condos, lobbies, restaurants and terraces.

Stainless steel is the material for spaces that need to look sharp for years with almost no maintenance. We TIG-weld and finish stainless posts and handrails in our shop, then fit cable, rod or glass infill on site.

For buildings near the water or anything exposed to salt, we use 316 stainless, which resists the brown 'tea staining' that cheaper 304 can show outdoors.

What we do: stainless steel & glass railings

Systems

  • Stainless posts with cable infill
  • Stainless posts with tempered or laminated glass
  • Stainless round-bar and flat-bar railings
  • Standoff-mounted glass with stainless cap rail

Handrails

  • Round stainless handrails on brackets
  • LED-channel handrails
  • Stainless grab bars and ADA rails

Finishes

  • #4 brushed
  • Mirror-polished accents
  • Black-finished stainless

Stainless Steel & Glass Railings FAQ

Is cable railing legal in NYC?

Cable infill can meet code when cables are tensioned and spaced so a 4-inch sphere can't pass, but horizontal infill is not allowed in every application. We'll confirm for your space before you pick a design.

Will stainless rust?

Stainless can show surface staining outdoors, especially 304 near salt. Using 316 and cleaning it a couple of times a year keeps it looking new.

Can you do a glass railing on a staircase?

Yes — raked glass panels follow the stair pitch with stainless posts or base shoes and a continuous handrail.

Do you offer free estimates?

Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

What areas do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.
