Stairs & Railings · Brooklyn & NYC

Spiral stairs for tight NYC footprints.

When a straight stair won't fit, a spiral will. We build steel spiral staircases for duplexes, roof access, lofts and storefront mezzanines — sized to the inch.

  • Measured on site
  • Built in our Brooklyn shop

Spiral stairs are how New York gets to the roof deck, the sleeping loft and the basement office without giving up a whole room. A well-built spiral feels solid underfoot; a cheap kit wobbles and rings. The difference is a properly sized center column, welded tread arms and a continuous handrail.

We fabricate the column, treads and helical rail in our shop to your exact floor-to-floor height and rotation, then bring it in through whatever door or window the building allows.

What we do: spiral staircases

Where we install them

  • Duplex apartments and penthouses
  • Roof-deck and bulkhead access
  • Loft and mezzanine access
  • Storefront basements and back offices

Tread options

  • Diamond plate (exterior, no-slip)
  • Solid steel plate with grip tape or rubber
  • Wood-capped steel pans
  • Perforated or bar-grating treads

Rails & balusters

  • Continuous rolled helical handrail
  • Vertical pickets spaced for code
  • Cable or round-bar infill
  • Stainless or wood handrail caps

Spiral Staircases FAQ

What diameter spiral stair do I need?

Most homes use 4 ft to 6 ft diameters. Smaller saves space but feels tighter to climb; we'll measure the opening and recommend the largest diameter that comfortably fits.

Can a spiral staircase be the only stair to a floor?

That depends on the use and code. Spirals are commonly allowed for access within a single dwelling unit and for roof access, but not always as a primary exit. We'll flag this before you commit.

Can you build an outdoor spiral stair to a roof deck?

Yes — exterior spirals are one of our most common requests. We galvanize or powder coat them and use slip-resistant treads.

Do you offer free estimates?

Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

What areas do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.
