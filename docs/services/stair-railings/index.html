Stairs & Railings · Brooklyn & NYC

Stair railings that finish the room.

Modern flat-bar, classic iron pickets, cable rail and wall-mounted handrails — custom fabricated for brownstones, condos, lofts and offices.

A railing is safety equipment that happens to be the most visible piece of design in a stairwell. We build both halves of that job: guards and handrails at code height with the right baluster spacing, in a design that suits the house — whether that's a clean blackened-steel flat bar for a gut renovation or hand-forged pickets to match a 1900s brownstone.

Every railing is templated on site. Stairs in older Brooklyn homes wind, lean and change pitch, so we fit each section to the actual stair rather than forcing a straight kit onto it.

What we do: interior stair railings & handrails

Styles

  • Modern flat-bar and square-bar pickets
  • Horizontal bar and cable infill
  • Traditional wrought iron with scrolls and collars
  • Glass-panel railings with steel or stainless posts
  • Perforated and laser-cut panel railings

Handrails

  • Wall-mounted steel and stainless handrails
  • Continuous wrap-around rails at landings
  • Wood handrail caps on steel balustrades
  • ADA-style returns and extensions

Where we install

  • Brownstone and townhouse stairs
  • Condo and co-op common stairs
  • Loft and duplex stairs
  • Office, restaurant and retail stairs

Why Brooklyn chooses Angir for interior stair railings & handrails

01

Templated to your stair

Winding and out-of-plumb stairs are the norm in pre-war buildings. We template every run so posts land solidly and rails follow the true pitch.

02

Code height & spacing

We build guards and handrails to NYC Building Code heights and keep openings tight enough that a 4-inch sphere can't pass through.

03

Clean, quiet install

Welding and grinding happen in our shop. On site we fit, fasten and touch up — minimal mess in a finished home.

Interior Stair Railings & Handrails FAQ

How tall does a stair railing need to be in NYC?

Handrails on stairs are typically 34–38 inches above the tread nosing, and guards at open edges and landings are typically 42 inches in most occupancies (36 inches can apply inside some dwelling units). We confirm the exact requirement for your building type before fabricating.

Can you match an existing antique iron railing?

Yes. We can copy picket patterns, scrolls, collars and newel posts to extend or repair an original railing so the new section blends in.

Can you put a railing on a stair that has carpet or finished wood?

Yes. We use base plates and fasteners sized for the substrate and protect finished surfaces during install.

Do you offer free estimates?

Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

What areas do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.
