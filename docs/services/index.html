The Angir method

From photo to finished steel.

Six steps, one shop, one crew. You always know what's happening and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Call or text photos

    Send a few photos and rough measurements. Most jobs get a price range the same day.

  2. 02

    We measure on site

    We laser-measure and template the real opening, stair or wall — Brooklyn buildings are never square.

  3. 03

    Drawing & fixed quote

    You approve a simple drawing, material and finish before any steel is cut. No surprises.

  4. 04

    Built in our shop

    Cut, fit and welded in our Dyker Heights shop by the same crew that installs it.

  5. 05

    Finished to last

    Primer and industrial paint, powder coat or hot-dip galvanizing — chosen for NYC weather.

  6. 06

    Install & walkthrough

    Clean, fast install. We check every weld, latch and anchor with you before we leave.
