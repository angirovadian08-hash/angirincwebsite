Stairs & Railings · Brooklyn & NYC

Handrails that make access easy.

Ramp railings, continuous stair handrails and grab rails for homes, storefronts, offices and houses of worship — sized for a sure grip and built to stay put.

  • Measured on site

Whether a family member needs a sturdier rail on the front steps or a business needs accessible handrails on a new ramp, the details matter: graspable rail size, continuous runs, extensions at the top and bottom, and solid anchoring.

We fabricate in steel or stainless and install on concrete, brick, brownstone and wood framing.

What we do: ada handrails & grab rails

What we install

  • Wheelchair ramp railings (both sides)
  • Continuous stair handrails with extensions
  • Front-step and stoop grab rails
  • Wall-mounted handrails in hallways
  • Bathroom-adjacent grab bars (mounted to blocking)

Where

  • Homes and two-family houses
  • Storefronts and restaurants
  • Medical offices and schools
  • Churches, synagogues and mosques

Why Brooklyn chooses Angir for ada handrails & grab rails

01

Proper grip size

We use round or graspable profiles in the standard 1¼"–2" range so the rail can actually be held.

02

Extensions and returns

Rails extend past the top and bottom steps and return to the wall or post so sleeves can't catch.

03

Fast scheduling

Simple handrails are often measured and installed within days.

How it works

  1. 1. Call or text photos

    Send a few photos and rough measurements. Most jobs get a price range the same day.

  2. 2. We measure on site

    We laser-measure and template the real opening, stair or wall — Brooklyn buildings are never square.

  3. 3. Drawing & fixed quote

    You approve a simple drawing, material and finish before any steel is cut. No surprises.

  4. 4. Built in our shop

    Cut, fit and welded in our Dyker Heights shop by the same crew that installs it.

  5. 5. Finished to last

    Primer and industrial paint, powder coat or hot-dip galvanizing — chosen for NYC weather.

  6. 6. Install & walkthrough

    Clean, fast install. We check every weld, latch and anchor with you before we leave.

ADA Handrails & Grab Rails FAQ

Can you add a handrail to my parent's front steps quickly?

Yes. Simple straight handrails are one of our fastest jobs — often measured and installed within the same week.

Do you build wheelchair ramps too?

We fabricate steel ramp frames and railings. For concrete ramps we install the railings once the concrete is poured.

Will an ADA rail meet my building's inspection?

We build to the standard accessible-rail dimensions. If your project has drawings or an inspector's checklist, send it over and we'll build to it exactly.

Do you offer free estimates?

Send us photos and rough measurements by text or email and we'll usually give you a price range quickly. For custom work we'll visit to measure.

What areas do you serve?

All of Brooklyn, plus Staten Island, Queens and Manhattan. South Brooklyn neighborhoods are closest to our shop.
